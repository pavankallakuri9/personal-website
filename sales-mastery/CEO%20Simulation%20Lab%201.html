How the Executive Arena works

This is a five-year, round-based management simulation. You lead Aureon Industrial Systems, a manufacturer operating two facilities and selling three industrial pump families.

  1. Read the Situation Room for market sizes, competitor behavior and the board's mandate.
  2. Open CEO Decisions, select all seven strategy domains and enter your assumptions. Numerical controls have explicit boundaries.
  3. Review live Financial Command forecasts. The model applies demand elasticity, capacity limits, headcount, material inflation, channel discounting, inventory, credit terms, borrowing, investment and taxes.
  4. Open Board Assessment to see provisional scores and constraint warnings.
  5. Commit to lock the year. The following year's opening cash, debt, workforce, assets and market positions carry forward.

Fair play: competing scenarios share preconfigured annual events. Previewing decisions does not consume a round; committed rounds cannot be undone. You can save/load a JSON game file or restart entirely.

Model boundary: all companies and figures are fictional. This is an advanced teaching instrument, not a validated valuation model or a Harvard-endorsed case.
