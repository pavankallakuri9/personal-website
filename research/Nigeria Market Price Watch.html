What the numbers can and can't say

📏 Pack sizes were resized

  • Scotch bonnet and bell pepper: 2025 30 kg bag scaled to 25 kg.
  • Onions: 2025 50 kg bag scaled to 100 kg.
  • Long dry pepper: 2025 100 kg bag scaled to 50 kg.
  • Resized items carry a RESIZED tag. Grade or quality may differ.

🚫 Left out of the year-on-year view

  • Maize, millet and soyabean: 2025 split by harvest or colour; 2026 did not.
  • Yellow maize and short dry pepper: no 2025 quote.
  • Egusi and dried fish: no 2026 quote at the four shared markets.

⚠️ Keep in mind

  • Eggs are quoted per crate in both years; crate size is not printed, so treat that change as provisional.
  • The 2025 survey covered north-west markets only. Don't read the four-market changes as a national trend.
  • Figures are transcribed from photographed bulletins and have not been verified against live markets.
